Service · AI & ERP Integration

AI and ERP integration — without replacing your ERP.

Built-N-AI connects practical AI and automation to the ERP you already run — adding modules and workflows that write clean data into it, and giving authorized users plain-English answers from it — instead of starting over with a new system.

Every case study runs inside an ERP.

Attribute research tool

An AI-driven Dolibarr tool that researches, validates and scores product attributes, then pushes updates to marketplace listings.

Warehouse execution

Paperless picking, QC and shipping workflows built on Dolibarr with live status and a full audit trail.

Ask Your Business

Authorized superusers ask ERP, inventory, sales and operational data questions in plain English; a language model translates them into real database queries.

How we keep it safe

Controls are part of the design.

  • Read-only access where a tool only needs to answer questions
  • Permissions and sensitive-data controls
  • Guardrails that keep each tool inside an approved scope
  • Human-in-the-loop review before anything that needs judgment reaches the ERP
  • Validation and a complete audit trail of who did what and when
