Dolibarr vs Acumatica · ERP comparison · Updated October 2026

Dolibarr vs Acumatica: open-source ERP or resource-priced cloud ERP?

Both let you add users without paying per seat. Only one has no subscription at all. This is a one-to-one comparison of what each includes, what each costs and where each one fits, written by an operator who runs Dolibarr with 26 custom modules in a live distribution business.

The short answer

Dolibarr if you want to own the system and skip the subscription. Acumatica if you want a vendor-run cloud ERP and a reseller to run it.

Acumatica does not charge per user. It prices by the applications you turn on, your transaction volume and how you deploy it, and it sells only through resellers, so there is no public price list. Warehouse management with barcoding, field service, payroll, fixed assets and multi-currency are add-ons on top of the edition you buy.

Dolibarr is released under the GPL v3 or later. There is no subscription and no paid edition: stock, barcodes, lot and serial tracking, manufacturing, double-entry accounting and the REST API are all in the free core. You pay for implementation, support and hosting, and the money you would have spent on a subscription goes into modules that fit your operation.

Honest guidance

When Acumatica is the right call.

You run several legal entities

Acumatica offers intercompany accounting as an add-on. Dolibarr needs an external module for multi-company.

You need advanced manufacturing planning

Acumatica has advanced planning and scheduling, a product configurator and estimating. Dolibarr’s core stops at bills of materials and manufacturing orders.

You want vendor-built AI and a native mobile app

Acumatica ships AI Studio, an AI assistant and mobile apps. Dolibarr’s AI uses your own provider key, and it is a responsive web app.

You want a reseller to own the relationship

Acumatica sells and supports through 350+ resellers. Dolibarr’s US partner base is small, which is why we document everything we build.

Our rule of thumb. If a five-figure annual subscription and a multi-month reseller project fit your budget and you want the vendor to carry the platform, Acumatica is a strong product. If you would rather put that money into modules that remove labor from your warehouse, and own the result, Dolibarr wins on economics.

Questions buyers ask

Dolibarr vs Acumatica, answered plainly.

Does Acumatica really include unlimited users?

Yes. Acumatica says you pay for the applications you use and your transaction volume, not for user seats. One research site reports that its entry tier is sized for about 10 named users and 1,000 transactions a month, so confirm the limits in your own quote. Dolibarr has no user limit and no license fee.

How much does Acumatica cost?

Acumatica does not publish prices; every quote comes through a reseller. Third-party estimates put a mid-market, single-entity subscription with 3 to 5 modules at about $25,000 to $60,000 a year, with implementation of about $40,000 to $150,000 and a typical timeline of 4 to 8 months. Dolibarr has no license or subscription fee; you pay for implementation, support and hosting.

Is Dolibarr really free?

Yes. Dolibarr is released under the GNU GPL v3 or later, with no paid edition, no per-user fee and no limit on users, machines or features. You pay for hosting, implementation and any custom work, not for the software.

How many companies use Acumatica and Dolibarr?

Acumatica states it has more than 10,000 customers. The Dolibarr project estimates about 200,000 active organizations worldwide, based on install data. The figures are not like-for-like: Acumatica counts paying customers, while Dolibarr's number is an estimate that includes many very small companies.

Does Acumatica include warehouse management?

Acumatica includes inventory management from its Distribution edition up, but warehouse management with barcoding is an optional add-on. Dolibarr includes multi-warehouse stock, barcodes and lot and serial tracking in its free core; Built-N-AI adds bin locations, paperless picking, quality control and returns as custom modules.

Who owns Acumatica?

Acumatica, based in Bellevue, Washington, signed an agreement on 29 May 2025 to be acquired by Vista Equity Partners from EQT. Dolibarr is an open-source project governed by its community under the GPL.

Does Dolibarr have AI like Acumatica?

Dolibarr includes a free AI module for text generation, summaries and translation that uses your own AI provider key, and Dolibarr 24 adds an experimental AI assistant and MCP server for connecting AI agents. Acumatica offers vendor-built AI, including AI Studio and an AI assistant.
