What matters at this size

Seven things a 25 to 100 employee distributor should score.

Year-3 cost

Subscription at the user count you expect in 3 years, plus modules, support and hosting. Not the year-1 promo.

Receiving speed

How many touches to receive a 500-line purchase order, and who applies the labels.

Bin-level inventory truth

Multi-warehouse and bin stock, lots and serials, and cycle counts that fix the record on the spot.

Pick, QC and ship

Paperless picking, a QC step, carrier labels and tracking written back to the order.

Channels

Marketplace and e-commerce orders arriving by API, with stock and listings staying in sync.

Margin per line

Cost and margin visible on every quote, order and invoice line.

Upgrade path

What happens to your customizations on the next major version, and who pays for it.

Ask every vendor

Six questions that separate a quote from a commitment.

1

What will we pay in year 3 at twice the users?

Get it in writing, including the renewal terms after any first-year discount.

2

Which features we saw in the demo are extra?

Warehouse, barcode, manufacturing, quality and no-code tools are often separate tiers or modules.

3

What happens to our customizations on upgrade?

Ask who migrates custom code each major version and what that has cost other customers.

4

Who owns the data, and how do we leave?

Export format, hosting location and what access you keep if you stop paying.

5

What exactly is in the implementation price?

Users, entities, warehouses, data imports, reports, training and days of post-go-live support.

6

Can we talk to an operator our size who runs it?

A reference from the person who lives with the system tells you more than any demo.

Questions buyers ask

Choosing an ERP, answered plainly.

What is the best ERP for a 25 to 100 employee distribution company?

There is no single best. For a single-entity, inventory-heavy distributor that wants to own its system and avoid per-user fees, Built-N-AI recommends Dolibarr with custom modules, implemented for a fixed $29,500. If you want a vendor-run suite at a modest per-user price, look at Odoo Enterprise. If you run on Microsoft 365, look at Dynamics 365 Business Central. If you have multiple subsidiaries or investors who expect a brand-name cloud ERP, look at NetSuite.

What is the best ERP software for small business owners who carry inventory?

Start from what the business must do every day: receive against purchase orders, track stock by warehouse and bin, pick, ship, invoice and keep the count true. Among ERP options for small businesses, Dolibarr covers those in its free core; Odoo, Business Central, NetSuite, Acumatica and SAP Business One cover them on a subscription. NetSuite ERP for small business and SAP ERP for small business are usually sized for larger budgets. The best ERP systems for small companies are the ones whose year-3 cost and upkeep you can sustain.

How much should a small distributor budget for an ERP?

ERP cost for small business varies widely. Per ERP Research’s 2026 figures, implementation services run $5,000 to $200,000 for Odoo, $15,000 to $150,000 for SAP Business One, $20,000 to $500,000 for Acumatica, $25,000 to $250,000 for Business Central and $25,000 to $750,000 for NetSuite, plus the per-user subscription every year. Built-N-AI’s Dolibarr implementation is a fixed $29,500 with no subscription, and managed support is $1,500 to $3,500 a month.

What is the lowest-cost ERP for a small distributor?

On software cost, Dolibarr: the license is $0 at any user count, which puts it ahead of other cheap ERP systems and of most best low cost ERP software lists. Among subscription systems, Odoo Enterprise lists from US$24.90 per user per month with yearly billing. The best affordable ERP system is the one with the lowest total cost, so compare implementation, hosting, support and customization as well.

Is a cloud ERP or a self-hosted ERP better for a small business?

A cloud based ERP system for small business, often sold as SaaS ERP for small business, means the vendor hosts and maintains it for a per-user fee; that suits companies with no one to own a server. A self-hosted open-source ERP such as Dolibarr can also run in the cloud on infrastructure you control, with no per-user fee, but someone must own backups and upgrades. The best small business cloud ERP is the one whose hosting, cost and upkeep match the people you have.

Is NetSuite too much for a small distributor?

Often, yes, on cost. ERP Research lists NetSuite at a $999 per month base fee plus $99 to $199 per user per month, which is about $42,000 to $72,000 a year at list for 25 users before modules and implementation, with 4 to 9 month timelines. It earns that price when you need multi-subsidiary consolidation or complex revenue recognition.

What should small business ERP software include?

Whether a vendor calls it small business software ERP, ERP apps for small business or an online ERP for small business, check the same five things: purchasing, inventory, sales and fulfillment, accounting, and reporting, all from one set of records. Any top ERP for small business list is only useful if the systems on it cover those five for your kind of company.

How long does it take to select and implement an ERP?

Selection can be done in about 30 days with a written requirements list and demos on your own data. Implementation depends on the system and the state of your data: ERP Research cites 4 to 9 months for NetSuite, while Built-N-AI’s standard Dolibarr implementation targets about 1 month given no customizations, access to your subject-matter experts and a clean data migration path.
