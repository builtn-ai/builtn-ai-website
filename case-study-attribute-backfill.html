AI product data case study

300,000 parts, 9 days, 98% accuracy.

A backlog no one could justify staffing for manually — solved by an automated tool that keeps running on every new part.

The challenge

A backlog nobody was ever staffed to fix.

Parts existed in the ERP with only 4–5 attributes each, and no reliable way to fill in the rest at scale. This wasn’t a slow process before — it was a problem that had never been solved, so no team was ever built to attempt it manually.

Doing it by hand would have taken about 5 minutes per part number to research and populate the missing attributes. Across the full database, that math never justified staffing the work.

The solution

A tool that researches, validates and scores every field.

We built an AI-driven Dolibarr tool that cross-references OEM sites, eBay, Amazon and other spec sources for each part, dedupes and validates the data across multiple sources, and returns every field with a confidence score on a review dashboard.

After about a week of tuning for AI drift and false positives, it reached 98% accuracy — high enough that no manual check was required. It was then run against the full database.

What it returns per part

  • Cross-referenced OEM, eBay, Amazon and other spec sources
  • Deduped and validated across multiple sources
  • A confidence score per field
  • Auto-pushed to every marketplace listing the same day
300,000 parts backfilled in 9 days. Equivalent to about 25,000 hours — roughly $625,000 — of manual research labor at $25/hour and 5 minutes per part. Work this company was never able to justify staffing for by hand.

What happens next

Permanent, not a one-time cleanup.

The same trigger system that ran the backfill keeps running: every new part gets the same research, validation and confidence scoring, and every update pushes to marketplace listings automatically. Part creation through marketplace listing update is now fully automatic.
