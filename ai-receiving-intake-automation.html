Service · Receiving & Intake Automation

AI receiving and intake automation.

Built-N-AI builds receiving and intake workflows where a unit is handled once: photographed, identified by AI, reviewed on a screen and received into your ERP — replacing manual research, hand-keyed product records and one-by-one barcode entry.

The problem

Receiving is where labor, time and bad data pile up.

Multiple touches per unit

Reading packaging, cross-referencing part numbers, researching manufacturer data and hand-keying the record — each step a chance for error.

Weeks to sale-ready

In the AI Intake case study, a 2,000-unit truckload took 3–4 weeks from receipt to sale-ready.

Repeated motions

In the bulk receiving case study, a 500-line PO took one person about 4 hours of scan-and-apply, even with a known manifest.

What we build

Two proven intake workflows.

AI product identification

Photos of the box and unit go to a multimodal AI step that returns manufacturer part number, manufacturer, specifications, a marketplace-ready title, UPC, MSRP, used market price and a confidence rate in 15–30 seconds. A person reviews on screen; one click creates missing product records and the purchase order.

Single-touch bulk receiving

Enter the first barcode and the rest of the PO auto-sequences with labels auto-printing, or upload a spreadsheet to auto-receive the full PO. Not every fix needs AI — this one removes a repeated motion.
