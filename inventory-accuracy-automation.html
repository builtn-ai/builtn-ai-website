Service · Inventory Accuracy & Reconciliation

Inventory accuracy and reconciliation automation.

Built-N-AI builds barcode-driven cycle-counting workflows that compare what is physically in a bin to what the ERP expects and reconcile the difference automatically — no separate cleanup step, and no phantom inventory promised to customers.

The problem

Counting is easy. Reconciling is the hard part.

The difficult work is resolving discrepancies: where missing units belong in the system, whether they stay sellable, and how to correct units that are physically in one bin but expected in another. In the case study, most of that was manual.

What we build

The employee captures reality. The system reconciles itself.

Scan the bin

An employee scans every unit present — no printed list to compare.

Missing units held

Anything expected but not scanned moves to non-sellable holding immediately.

Mislocated units corrected

Anything scanned but expected elsewhere has its ERP location updated on the spot.

Connected to picking

Picking overrides feed the same workflow, so exceptions found on the floor get reconciled, with every adjustment logged.
