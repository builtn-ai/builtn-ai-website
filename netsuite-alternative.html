An open-source NetSuite alternative for distributors who want the capability without the per-user bill.

NetSuite ERP is serious software with a serious price: a base fee, a per-user fee, module fees and a five-figure implementation before the first order ships. If you are a single-entity inventory business between spreadsheets and that bill, here is what Dolibarr, implemented and extended by Built-N-AI, does instead, and where NetSuite is still the right answer. The short answer

Same jobs done. One line missing from the invoice.

ERP Research lists Oracle NetSuite at a $999 per month base platform fee plus $99 to $199 per user per month, with CRM, e-commerce, manufacturing and warehouse modules priced separately and implementations from $25,000 to $750,000. Their typical figures: $12,000 to $60,000 a year for a 10-user core deployment and $120,000 to $300,000 or more a year for a 50-user rollout with ERP, CRM and e-commerce, on 4 to 9 month timelines.

Dolibarr is GPL open source. Purchasing, multi-warehouse inventory, lot and serial tracking, sales, shipping, manufacturing, double-entry accounting, CRM and a REST API are in the free core. Built-N-AI implements it for a fixed $29,500, typically live in about 1 month, and extends it with the modules that make it run a real warehouse. The license line is $0 at 10 users or 100.

Honest guidance

When NetSuite is the right call.

Multiple subsidiaries

Consolidation across entities and currencies is native in NetSuite; Dolibarr needs an external module for multi-company.

Complex revenue recognition

Subscriptions, milestones and ASC 606 schedules are a NetSuite strength.

Investor or acquirer expectation

Some boards and buyers want a brand-name cloud ERP on the books. That is a legitimate reason.

You want one vendor to own it

Hosting, upgrades, mobile apps and support from a single large vendor, at a per-seat price.

Our rule of thumb. If your business is one entity, lives on inventory, and the NetSuite quote is a real stretch, Dolibarr with the right modules does the job and you keep the difference every year. If you need multi-subsidiary finance or complex revenue recognition, pay for NetSuite and do not look back.

Questions buyers ask

NetSuite alternatives, answered plainly.

What is the best open-source alternative to NetSuite?

For small and middle-market inventory businesses, Dolibarr: GPL open source with purchasing, multi-warehouse inventory, lot and serial tracking, sales, shipping, manufacturing, double-entry accounting, CRM and a REST API in the free core, extended by Built-N-AI’s modules for AI intake, paperless warehouse execution, cycle counting, marketplace orders and one-touch shipping. Running today with more than 400,000 SKUs and no per-user fees.

Can Dolibarr replace NetSuite?

For a single-entity inventory business of roughly 5 to 100 users, yes. NetSuite remains the better fit for multi-subsidiary consolidation, complex revenue recognition, or companies that want a single vendor-run cloud suite with a large partner network.

How much does NetSuite cost compared with Dolibarr?

ERP Research lists NetSuite at a $999 per month base fee plus $99 to $199 per user per month, modules extra, implementation $25,000 to $750,000; about $12,000 to $60,000 a year for 10 users and $120,000 to $300,000 or more for 50. Dolibarr’s license is $0; Built-N-AI’s implementation is a fixed $29,500 and managed support is $1,500 to $3,500 a month.

What do you give up by leaving NetSuite?

Native multi-subsidiary consolidation, built-in advanced revenue recognition, a vendor-run cloud with native mobile apps, and the SuiteApp and partner ecosystem. In exchange you own the software and data, pay no per-user fees, host where you choose, and customize through modules without losing the upgrade path.

How long does it take to move from NetSuite to Dolibarr?

About 1 month for the standard package, assuming no customizations, access to your subject-matter experts and a clean data migration path. NetSuite data complexity can extend that; the formal schedule is confirmed after due diligence.
