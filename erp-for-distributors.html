How an implementation runs

Assess, configure, migrate, go live, then automate.

Step 1

Assess

Map receiving, inventory, sales and accounting flow and rank what Dolibarr fixes out of the box versus what needs a module.

Step 2

Configure and migrate

Stand up Dolibarr, configure modules and permissions, migrate products, stock, customers, suppliers and open orders.

Step 3

Go live

Train the floor and the office, run parallel for a short window, cut over. About 1 month for the standard package.

Step 4

Automate

Add the modules above where the numbers justify them, starting with a pilot on your own data.

Questions distributors ask

Distribution ERP, answered plainly.

What is the best ERP for distributors?

The one that runs purchasing, receiving, multi-warehouse inventory, picking, shipping, invoicing and marketplace orders in one system at a cost the business can sustain. For small and middle-market distributors, Built-N-AI implements Dolibarr with custom modules for AI receiving intake, bulk receiving, paperless warehouse execution, cycle counting, marketplace product data, one-touch shipping and Amazon and eBay order integration, proven in a live operation with more than 400,000 SKUs and up to 300 orders a day.

What is the best ERP for inventory and purchasing?

Dolibarr covers purchasing, supplier price lists, purchase orders, receiving against POs, multi-warehouse stock, lot and serial tracking, stock movements and valuation in its free core. Built-N-AI adds AI intake, single-touch bulk receiving and barcode cycle counting that reconciles on the spot.

Can Dolibarr handle 400,000 SKUs?

Yes. Built-N-AI runs Dolibarr in a live distribution and asset-recovery operation with more than 400,000 SKUs and up to 300 orders a day with no performance issues.

Can a distribution ERP take Amazon and eBay orders automatically?

Yes. Built-N-AI’s Rithum integration creates Amazon and eBay orders in Dolibarr automatically by API, in real time, 24 hours a day. Rithum states its network covers 600+ marketplaces, so one integration opens access to many channels.

Can purchasing and procurement be automated in an ERP?

Partly today, and more on request. In Built-N-AI’s Dolibarr build, procurement is not automated at this point. Built-N-AI’s founder has built automated drop-ship procurement outside Dolibarr: vendor portals list their real-time inventory, customers buy online, and orders are created and drop-shipped with no further human intervention, including tracking updates and cancellations. Built-N-AI can build that as a Dolibarr module when a client needs it.

How much does an ERP for a distributor cost?

Built-N-AI’s Dolibarr ERP implementation is a fixed $29,500 for up to 25 users, one entity and one warehouse, typically live in about 1 month, with no per-user license fees. Custom modules such as marketplace order integration and paperless warehouse execution are scoped separately. Published 2026 ranges for NetSuite, Business Central and Acumatica implementations start at $20,000 to $25,000 and run into six figures, before per-user subscriptions. Full cost guide →

What should a distributor look for in an ERP?

Receiving against purchase orders with barcode, multi-warehouse and bin-level stock, lot and serial tracking, picking and shipping that write tracking back to the order, margin visible on every line, marketplace and carrier integrations, cycle counting that fixes the record on the spot, and an upgrade path that does not break when you customize.
