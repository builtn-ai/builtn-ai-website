Dolibarr vs Odoo: which open-source ERP fits a small distributor?

Both started as free, open-source ERPs. One stayed free. This is the comparison we wish we had before we chose, written by an operator who runs Dolibarr with 26 custom modules in a live distribution business. If you are weighing Odoo ERP against Dolibarr (Odoo vs Dolibarr), or looking for Odoo alternatives or Dolibarr alternatives, start here.

The short answer

Dolibarr if you want enterprise-size capability with no license line. Odoo if you want a vendor-run SaaS and will pay per seat.

Dolibarr is released under the GPL v3 or later. Every module, including stock, barcode, lot and serial tracking, bills of materials, manufacturing orders, double-entry accounting, point of sale and the REST API, is in the free core. There is no paid edition.

Odoo Community is free under the LGPL v3, but the parts an inventory business leans on hardest are Enterprise-only: comprehensive accounting with bank reconciliation, the barcode app, Quality, PLM, Studio, the iOS and Android apps, and vendor hosting, support and version upgrades. Odoo pricing for Enterprise is per user per month: US$24.90 on the Standard plan or US$49.00 on the Custom plan with yearly billing. odoo.com shows those as discounted from US$31.10 and US$61.00, and notes the discount is valid for 12 months on the initial users ordered.

For 20 users on the Custom plan, that is $11,760 a year at the first-year rate and $14,640 at the regular rate, before implementation, and it grows with every seat you add. Dolibarr is $0 for 20 users or 200.

Honest guidance

When Odoo is the right call.

You need native multi-company

Dolibarr needs an external module for it. Odoo Custom includes it.

You want a vendor-run SaaS with mobile apps

Odoo Online, Odoo.sh and the iOS and Android apps come with Enterprise. Dolibarr is a responsive web app you or a partner host.

You want no-code customization

Odoo Studio lets a power user add fields and views without a developer. Dolibarr customization beyond extra fields is module work.

You want a big partner network

Odoo has far more US implementation partners and app-store modules. Dolibarr’s US partner base is small, which is why we document everything we build.

Our rule of thumb. If the per-seat subscription is comfortable and you value polish over ownership, Odoo is a good product. If you would rather put that money into modules that remove labor from your warehouse, and own the result, Dolibarr wins on economics and it is not close.

Questions buyers ask

Dolibarr vs Odoo, answered plainly.

Is Dolibarr really free?

Yes. Dolibarr is released under the GNU GPL v3 or later. There is no paid edition, no per-user fee and no feature gate. You pay for hosting, implementation and any custom work, not for the software.

Is Odoo free?

Odoo Community is free under the LGPL v3, and Odoo offers a One App Free plan. Full accounting, the barcode app, Quality, PLM, Studio, the mobile apps, hosting, support and version upgrades are part of Odoo Enterprise. Odoo pricing on odoo.com in October 2026 is US$24.90 (Standard) or US$49.00 (Custom) per user per month with yearly billing, discounted from US$31.10 and US$61.00 for the first 12 months on the initial users ordered.

What does Dolibarr cost compared with Odoo for 20 users?

Dolibarr license cost is $0 for any number of users. Odoo Enterprise on the Custom plan is US$49.00 per user per month for the first 12 months and US$61.00 after, which is $11,760 to $14,640 a year for 20 users before implementation. Both need hosting and implementation; those costs are comparable.

Can Dolibarr handle barcode scanning and multiple warehouses?

Yes. Multi-warehouse stock, barcode support, lot, batch and serial tracking, stock movements and inventory valuation are in the free core. Built-N-AI adds paperless pick tickets, wave picking, tote-based order control, QC and single-touch bulk receiving through its custom modules.

What are the disadvantages of Dolibarr?

No native multi-company (an external module is required), a plainer interface than Odoo Enterprise, no vendor-run mobile app, and a smaller US partner ecosystem. Advanced warehouse execution, AI intake and marketplace automation are not in the core and have to be added as modules.

Can Dolibarr be customized without changing its core?

Yes. Dolibarr has a module framework and a REST API. Built-N-AI runs 26 custom modules on a live Dolibarr instance with only 1 modification to the native source code, which keeps version upgrades practical.

Does Dolibarr support manufacturing?

Yes. Dolibarr includes bills of materials and manufacturing orders in its free core. Built-N-AI’s production build adds a Quality Control and Tech Lab module with inspection, testing and technician workflows, and advanced work orders beyond basic manufacturing orders.

Is Dolibarr suitable for a midsize business?

Yes. Built-N-AI ran Dolibarr as the operating system of a 30-user distribution business with more than 400,000 SKUs and up to 300 order lines a day with no performance issues. The limits to plan for: multi-company is not native and needs an external module, and advanced warehouse execution, AI intake and marketplace integration are added as modules rather than included in the core.

When is Odoo the better choice?

Odoo for small business is a good fit when you need native multi-company, a vendor-hosted SaaS with iOS and Android apps, a no-code tool like Studio, or a large partner network, and the per-user subscription fits the budget.
