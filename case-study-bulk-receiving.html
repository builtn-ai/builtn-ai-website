Receiving automation case study

From one-by-one barcode entry to single-touch bulk receiving.

Removing one repeated motion from the receiving process cut a 500-line PO from 4 hours to 1, and labor cost 75%.

The challenge

One repeated motion, hundreds of times per PO.

Receiving a large purchase order meant scanning a barcode and applying it to a physical unit, one at a time, all the way through a PO that could run hundreds of lines. A 500-line PO took one person about 4 hours — even when every part number was already known and the manifest was accurate.

That labor cost added up fast, and it gave every receiver the same repetitive task: scan, apply, repeat, hundreds of times per PO.

The solution

Two paths, one touch per unit.

We eliminated one-by-one barcode entry with two paths to the same result, so a receiver never touches a unit twice.

A “magic wand” on the receiving screen: enter the first barcode, hit the wand, and it auto-populates the rest in sequence across the full PO. A receiver can override and restart the sequence anywhere it doesn’t match — labels auto-print at the same time, so each unit is touched once.

For POs that arrive as a file, a spreadsheet import option auto-receives the entire PO by upload — no barcode entry at all, with the same time savings.

Two paths, one touch

  • Enter the first barcode, hit the wand
  • Sequence auto-populates the rest of the PO
  • Override and restart the sequence anywhere
  • Label auto-prints as each unit sequences
  • Or: upload a spreadsheet, auto-receive the full PO
  • Either way — one touch per unit
500-line PO: 4 hours down to 1 hour. Labor cost per PO fell from about $100 to $25 — a 75% reduction — with labels already printed and no re-handling required.
