Questions buyers ask

Dolibarr ERP, answered plainly.

What is Dolibarr?

Dolibarr ERP CRM is free, open-source enterprise resource planning software, released under the GPL. Dolibarr software runs sales, purchasing, inventory, manufacturing, accounting and HR in one ERP system, with Dolibarr CRM covering prospects, opportunities and proposals, and it is extended with modules instead of changes to the core.

What is ERP software, in plain terms?

ERP stands for enterprise resource planning; that is the ERP meaning behind the acronym. What is an ERP system in practice? One system of record for buying, stocking, selling, shipping and accounting, replacing the separate ERP tools, apps and spreadsheets a growing company ends up re-keying between. Most ERP systems and ERP software solutions are sold per user per month; among ERP solutions, Dolibarr has no license fee.

ERP vs CRM: what is the difference?

A CRM manages customers, prospects and the sales pipeline. An ERP runs the operation behind the sale: purchasing, inventory, fulfillment, invoicing and accounting. Dolibarr includes both in one system, so a quote becomes an order, a shipment and an invoice without re-keying.

Is QuickBooks an ERP system?

QuickBooks is considered a lightweight ERP. It handles accounting well, and many small businesses run on it. It also struggles with transaction volume: in our experience it could not handle 100 purchase orders or sales orders a day. Companies typically move to a full ERP such as Dolibarr when purchasing, warehouse, inventory and fulfillment need to run in the same system as the books.

Is Dolibarr really free ERP software?

Yes. Dolibarr ERP pricing is simple: the license is free at any user count, which makes it a free ERP for small business and midsize companies alike. Dolibarr cost comes from implementation, monthly support and your own hosting, not from the software.

Can Dolibarr handle multiple warehouses and a large catalog?

Yes. Dolibarr inventory management covers multiple warehouses, stock movements, lot, batch and serial tracking and barcode support in the core. We ran it with more than 400,000 SKUs and up to 300 orders a day with no performance issues.

Is Dolibarr suitable for a midsize business?

Yes. We ran a 30-user warehouse and inventory operation on it. Plan for two limits: Dolibarr multicompany is not native and needs an external module, and advanced warehouse execution, AI intake and marketplace integration come from modules, which is what we built.

Does Dolibarr support manufacturing?

Yes. Dolibarr manufacturing covers bills of materials (Dolibarr BOM) and manufacturing orders in the core, and our Quality Control & Tech Lab module adds inspection, testing, technician workflows and advanced work orders.

What Dolibarr custom modules has Built-N-AI built?

Does Dolibarr integrate with e-commerce and marketplaces?

Yes. Dolibarr e-commerce and marketplace orders run through the ERP in our build. Our Dolibarr ecommerce integration work is in production: Amazon and eBay orders are created in the ERP by API in real time through Rithum, product data is pushed to marketplace listings the same day, and channel activity reconciles back to the ERP.
