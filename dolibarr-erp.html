Why Dolibarr

The ERP the big vendors do not want you to know about.

Most inventory businesses between $5M and $100M are stuck in the middle: too big for QuickBooks and spreadsheets, too small to justify a six-figure SAP, NetSuite or Dynamics project with per-seat fees that grow every year.

Dolibarr closes that gap. It is a mature, actively developed open-source ERP used by hundreds of thousands of companies worldwide, licensed under the GPL, with no per-user fees and no vendor lock-in. You own the software, you own your data, and you can host it anywhere.

Out of the box

What Dolibarr does the day you turn it on.

Enterprise functionality, module by module. Turn on what you need, leave the rest off.

Products & inventory

Product and service catalog, multi-warehouse stock, lot and serial tracking, stock movements with full history, min/max reorder levels and inventory valuation.

Purchasing

Supplier price lists, purchase orders, receiving against POs, supplier invoices and payments, with every receipt tied back to stock.

Sales & fulfillment

Quotes, sales orders, shipments, delivery notes and customer invoices, with margins visible on every line.

CRM

Customers, prospects, suppliers and contacts in one place, with opportunities, agenda, tasks and email tracking.

Accounting & bank

Double-entry accounting, chart of accounts, bank reconciliation, payment tracking, multi-currency and tax handling.

Manufacturing & projects

Bills of materials and manufacturing orders, project and task tracking with time and expenses billed back to the job.

HR & expenses

Employees, leave, expense reports and approvals, so the back office lives in the same system as the warehouse.

Open platform

Full REST API, role-based permissions, document generation and a module framework that lets us build exactly what your operation needs without breaking upgrades.

Built-N-AI custom modules

Where the real money is: the modules we built on top of Dolibarr.

Stock Dolibarr gets you a working ERP. These modules are what turned it into a warehouse that runs on data instead of paper. Each one was built inside a live operation and comes with a case study.

Module 1

AI Advanced Intake

What it does: Photograph a unit once. AI identifies the product and returns the manufacturer part number, specs, marketplace title, UPC, MSRP and resale price with a confidence score, then creates the product record and purchase order line in Dolibarr automatically.

Why it matters: Receiving unmanifested or mixed product by hand is the most expensive labor in the building. Receiving labor cost fell 81% ($23,500 to $4,500 per truckload) and time to sale-ready dropped from 3–4 weeks to 1 week.

AI Intake case study →

Module 2

Single-Touch Bulk Receiving

What it does: Enter the first barcode and hit the “magic wand” to auto-sequence the rest of the PO, with labels printing as each unit sequences. Or upload a spreadsheet and auto-receive the entire PO with no barcode entry at all.

Why it matters: A 500-line PO went from 4 hours to 1 hour and labor cost per PO fell 75%. Every unit is touched once.

Bulk Receiving case study →

Module 3

Digital Warehouse Execution

What it does: Paperless pick tickets, wave picking sequenced bin to bin, tote-based order control, Pass/Fail QC and shipping, and live status from Order Created to Shipped. A cancelled order cancels its pick ticket and blocks the tote from shipping.

Why it matters: Cancelled orders that shipped anyway went from ~20 a month to zero, avoiding ~$2,000 a month in lost product, with a full who/what/when audit trail.

Warehouse Execution case study →

Module 4

Real-Time Inventory Reconciliation

What it does: Barcode-driven cycle counting that compares every scan to the ERP and reconciles on the spot. Missing units move to non-sellable holding, mislocated units get their location corrected, and picking exceptions close out on the next count.

Why it matters: Manual list comparisons dropped to zero and inventory accuracy became closed-loop with picking. The employee captures reality; the system reconciles itself.

Inventory Reconciliation case study →

Module 5

AI Product Data & Marketplace Sync

What it does: Researches, validates and confidence-scores product attributes from OEM, eBay, Amazon and other sources, then pushes every update to marketplace listings the same day. Runs automatically on every new part.

Why it matters: 300,000 part records backfilled in 9 days at 98% accuracy, about $625,000 of manual research the business could never justify staffing.

Attribute Backfill case study →
