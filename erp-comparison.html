ERP comparison: Dolibarr vs Odoo vs Acumatica vs NetSuite.

If you are choosing an ERP for an inventory business, these are the four names that usually make the shortlist. This ERP system comparison puts them side by side on the same 66 features, how each is priced, and what each vendor can do to your price at renewal, written by an operator who runs Dolibarr with 26 custom modules in a live distribution business.

By Casey Jackson, operator and Dolibarr implementer · Updated 9 October 2026

Short answer. Dolibarr is the only one of the four with no license fee, unlimited users and full source code. Odoo publishes per-user prices. Acumatica prices by modules and transaction volume with unlimited users. NetSuite charges a base license plus per-user fees and paid modules. On our 66-feature checklist, Dolibarr covers 59, Odoo 54, NetSuite 48 and Acumatica 46.

Questions buyers ask

ERP comparison, answered plainly.

Which ERP is cheapest: Dolibarr, Odoo, Acumatica or NetSuite?

On license cost, Dolibarr: it is $0 under the GPL for any number of users. Odoo publishes $24.90 to $61.00 per user per month. Acumatica and NetSuite publish no prices; third-party estimates put a mid-market Acumatica subscription at about $25,000 to $60,000 a year, and NetSuite at a base license plus per-user fees and paid modules. Every system still needs implementation and support.

Which of these ERPs include unlimited users?

Dolibarr and Acumatica. Dolibarr has no user limit and no license fee. Acumatica prices by applications and transaction volume rather than seats. Odoo and NetSuite are priced per user; Odoo's One App Free plan has unlimited users but only one app.

Which ERP is best for a small business?

It depends on budget and how much you want to own. NetSuite ERP for small business and Acumatica's Small Business Edition suit companies that want a vendor-run cloud ERP and can carry an annual subscription. Odoo for small business works when a per-seat price is comfortable. Dolibarr fits inventory businesses that want no license fee and to own the system, with implementation from a partner such as Built-N-AI.

Can these ERP vendors raise their prices?

Dolibarr has no license fee to raise. Odoo raised its U.S. Custom plan list price from $46.70 to $61.00 per user per month between late 2025 and early 2026. Acumatica caps renewal increases at 10% a year in its April 2025 licensing guide. NetSuite publishes no cap; renewals are priced at Oracle's then-current pricing, and licensing advisors report first-renewal uplifts of about 10%.

What are the best Odoo alternatives and NetSuite alternatives?

Dolibarr is the open-source alternative we run: no license fee, unlimited users and full source code. Acumatica is the alternative if you want a vendor-run cloud ERP without per-user pricing. Each one-to-one comparison on this site covers the trade-offs in detail.

Which of these ERPs include warehouse management without paid add-ons?

Odoo's paid plans include its Barcode and Inventory apps. Acumatica and NetSuite sell warehouse management as an add-on. Dolibarr includes multi-warehouse stock, barcodes and lot and serial tracking in its free core, and Built-N-AI adds bin locations, paperless picking, quality control and returns as custom modules.
