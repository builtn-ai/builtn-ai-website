The short answer

Tens of thousands for the mid-market vendors, before the subscription. $29,500 fixed for Dolibarr, with no subscription.

ERP Research, an independent comparison service, publishes 2026 implementation ranges of $25,000 to $750,000 for Oracle NetSuite, $25,000 to $250,000 for Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, $20,000 to $500,000 for Acumatica, $15,000 to $150,000 for SAP Business One and $5,000 to $200,000 for Odoo. Those are services only. The per-user subscription is on top, every year: roughly $99 to $199 per user per month for NetSuite, $80 to $110 for Business Central, $95 to $250 for SAP Business One and from $24.90 for Odoo Enterprise, per the same source.

A 25-user company on a $100-per-user system pays about $30,000 a year in subscription alone. Over 3 years that is $90,000 before anyone has configured anything.

Dolibarr is GPL open source. The license line is $0 at 25 users or 250. That is why Built-N-AI can deliver a complete, production-ready implementation for a fixed $29,500 and still spend the budget where it matters: configuring an ERP that fits how your business actually runs.

What moves the price

Seven things that make any ERP implementation cost more.

User count

On subscription ERPs every seat is a recurring cost. On Dolibarr, users are free; only training time scales.

Entities and warehouses

Each additional company or location multiplies configuration, security and reporting work.

Data volume and cleanliness

Dirty part masters and duplicate customers are the most common cause of overruns. Fix them before import, not after.

Integrations

Marketplaces, carriers, e-commerce, accounting and EDI each add a scoped integration.

Custom workflows

Anything the native system does not do is a module. Keep them separate from core so upgrades stay clean.

Manufacturing and repair

Bills of materials, work orders, refurbishment and QC add process design and testing time.

Training and change

The floor has to use it on day one. Under-training is the cheapest way to fail an ERP project.
