The assessment

A written report you own, in one to two weeks.

We walk the work with the people who do it, look at the systems behind it, and hand you a document. It stands on its own — you can act on it yourself, take it to your own IT team, or take it to someone else.

  • Areas assessed — what we looked at and how
  • Findings and opportunities — the friction we found, with a rough cost on each
  • IT systems — what you run, how it connects, where the data actually lives
  • AI in use today — what your team has already tried, sanctioned or not
  • Key areas to address with AI — and, honestly, which ones AI is the wrong tool for
  • Prioritization list — ranked by value against effort, so you know what to do first
  • The outcome each one moves — every opportunity is tagged with which of the six outcomes it improves, and roughly by how much

How it typically goes

Call, assess, decide.

The call tells us whether there is anything here worth your money — if there is not, we will say so. The assessment gives you a ranked list of what to fix. Then you decide: run it yourself, bring us in to build and teach, or scope a single project. Nothing about the assessment obligates you to the next step.

Every assessment includes a 90-minute readout with you and your leadership team — we walk the findings, answer questions, and agree what matters most. Assessment clients also get first call on the next open start date and the current rate held for twelve months, whenever you choose to move.
