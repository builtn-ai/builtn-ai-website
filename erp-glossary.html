What is ERP? The meaning, in plain English.

ERP stands for enterprise resource planning. An enterprise resource planning system is one piece of software that runs purchasing, inventory, sales, shipping and accounting from a single set of records. If you want to define ERP in one line: it is the system the whole business runs on.

ERP definition

What ERP means, and what an ERP system does.

What does ERP mean, and what is enterprise resource planning? ERP means enterprise resource planning: planning and tracking the resources of a business, which in practice are its stock, money, orders and people. The ERP system meaning, and the ERP software meaning, is the same idea turned into software. ERP software, also written ERP system software or simply an ERP program, puts every department on one database so nobody re-keys what someone else already entered.

What are ERP systems used for? A purchase order is raised, goods are received against it, stock and cost update, a customer order is picked and shipped, the invoice posts to the ledger. In an ERP that is one chain of records. Without one it is four or five tools and a spreadsheet holding them together.

I have implemented full operations on both SAP and Dolibarr. Textbook ERP systems definitions run long. The ERP software definition that matters on the floor is simple: when the dock, the warehouse, sales and accounting all look at the same number, you have an ERP. When they each keep their own, you do not.

Questions people ask

ERP, answered plainly.

What does ERP stand for?

ERP stands for enterprise resource planning. The ERP acronym is the same in business and in software: what does ERP stand for in business and what does ERP stand for in software have one answer. The ERP full form is enterprise resource planning.

What is an ERP system?

An ERP system is one piece of software that runs the core of a business from a single database: purchasing, inventory, sales, shipping, invoicing and accounting. That is the ERP system definition in plain words. Because every department works from the same records, a receipt on the dock updates stock, cost and the books at the same moment.

What does ERP mean in business?

ERP meaning in business: the system of record for operations and finance. When people ask what is ERP in business, or what is an ERP system in business, the answer is the software where orders, stock, purchasing and accounting live together instead of in separate tools and spreadsheets.

What is an ERP system in accounting?

In accounting, an ERP system is the general ledger plus the operations that feed it. An ERP accounting system posts purchases, receipts, shipments and invoices to the books automatically, so finance does not re-key what operations already entered.

What are examples of ERP systems?

ERP system examples include SAP, Oracle NetSuite, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central, Acumatica, Sage, Infor, Epicor, Odoo, ERPNext and Dolibarr. The first three come from some of the biggest ERP companies and are among the most famous ERP software; Odoo, ERPNext and Dolibarr are open source ERP examples.

What is the difference between ERP and CRM?

ERP vs CRM: a CRM manages leads, contacts and the sales pipeline. An ERP manages what happens after the sale and behind it: purchasing, inventory, fulfillment, invoicing and accounting. Many ERP platforms, Dolibarr included, have a CRM module built in.

Is QuickBooks an ERP system?

QuickBooks is considered a lightweight ERP. It handles accounting well, and many small businesses run on it. It also struggles with transaction volume: in our experience it could not handle 100 purchase orders or sales orders a day. Companies typically move to a full ERP such as Dolibarr when purchasing, warehouse, inventory and fulfillment need to run in the same system as the books.

When does a small business need an ERP?

When the same data is being typed into more than one system, inventory in the books does not match the shelf, or month-end depends on spreadsheets. At that point an ERP solution replaces the re-keying with one record.
