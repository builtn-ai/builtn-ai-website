Free ERP for small business: what is really free, and what it costs to run.

There is a free ERP system for small business that is free at 5 users and at 500. There are also free plans that stop being free the day you grow. This page sorts the two, shows what a free ERP solution still costs to run, and what we ran on one: more than 400,000 SKUs with no per-user fees.

Read the word “free” carefully

Three kinds of free ERP.

1. Open source

Free for good

ERP open source free: the code is public, the license costs nothing. A free open source ERP for small business such as Dolibarr has no user limit. Dolibarr, Odoo Community and ERPNext are the names that come up for the best free open source ERP software.

2. Free tier

Free until you grow

A commercial product with a free plan, such as Odoo’s One App Free. Additional apps move you to a paid subscription.

3. Free trial

Free for a few weeks

Other vendors’ paid products marketed as a free cloud ERP for a trial period. When the trial ends, the subscription starts.

Searches for a free ERP program, ERP software freeware or ERP software open source free all land on these three. Only the first is free in the sense a growing business needs: no bill that scales with headcount.

Low cost ERP software is not the same as cheap ERP systems that struggle at volume. QuickBooks, in our experience, could not handle 100 purchase orders or sales orders a day. On Dolibarr we processed up to 300 orders a day efficiently, with no degradation and no bottlenecks at that volume. It also handled 2,000 lines on a single purchase order and received that volume in a single reception, using our custom modules. What makes a low cost ERP for small business work is removing the bill that grows with every hire, then spending once on a clean implementation: configuration, data migration and training. After go-live, the only cost to run it is your own servers or cloud hosting. That is how the best affordable ERP system ends up being a free one that was set up properly.

Questions buyers ask

Free ERP, answered plainly.

What is the best free ERP for small business?

For an inventory business that wants every module free with no user limit, Built-N-AI implements Dolibarr: GPL open source, one edition, nothing held back for a paid tier. Among the best free ERP software for small business, the other names to check are Odoo Community and ERPNext, compared on our open source ERP page.

Is Dolibarr free?

Yes. Dolibarr free means the whole product: it is published under the GPL v3 or later, there is one edition, and there are no per-user fees at any user count. Dolibarr cost comes from hosting, implementation, data migration, training and support, not from Dolibarr pricing, because there is no license price.

Is Odoo ERP free?

Partly. Odoo ERP free comes two ways: Odoo Community, the open source edition, and the One App Free plan on Odoo’s own cloud, where additional apps move you to a paid subscription. Odoo Inventory free is possible as that one app or in Community, but the Barcode app and comprehensive accounting are in paid Odoo Enterprise.

Is there a free cloud ERP for small business?

The software can be free; the cloud is not. A free cloud based ERP for small business usually means a free tier or a trial of a paid product. Dolibarr is web-based, so it runs as a free online ERP for small business with a $0 license. After go-live, the only cost to run it is your own servers, bought and hosted yourself, or your cloud hosting bill.

Is there free ERP software for inventory management?

Yes. Dolibarr’s free core includes multi-warehouse stock, barcode, lot and serial tracking, purchasing and receiving, so it works as free inventory management software and as free distribution software for small business. It also includes a double-entry ledger, which makes it free ERP accounting software as well.

What does a free ERP really cost?

The license is free. The project is not. Built-N-AI’s Dolibarr implementation is a fixed $29,500 for up to 25 users, one entity and one warehouse, typically live in about 1 month, with a formal price after due diligence. After go-live, the only cost to run it is your own servers or cloud hosting; there are no per-user license fees. Managed support is optional at $1,500 to $3,500 a month.

What is the cheapest ERP system for small business?

On license cost, the cheapest ERP system for small business is one with no license at all, which is what open source gives you. On total cost, the cheapest is the one that fits with the least custom work, so the best low cost ERP software depends on how close its core is to how you already operate.
