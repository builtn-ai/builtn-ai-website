Service · AI Product Data & Marketplace Automation

AI product data and marketplace automation.

Built-N-AI builds AI tools that research, validate and fill in missing product attributes across an entire catalog, score every field for confidence, and push updates to marketplace listings automatically — then keep running on every new part.

The problem

Thin product records cost you sales and never get fixed by hand.

In the attribute backfill case study, parts existed in the ERP with only 4–5 attributes each. Researching the rest by hand took about 5 minutes per part number — a backlog no team was ever staffed to attempt.

Incomplete records produce thinner marketplace listings, which affects impressions, conversion and sell-through.

What we build

Research, validate, score, sync.

Multi-source research

Cross-references OEM sites, eBay, Amazon and other spec sources for each part.

Validation and dedupe

Compares embeddings of spec data to find and merge the closest match across sources.

Confidence per field

Every field returns with a confidence score on a review dashboard.

Same-day marketplace sync

Updates push to every marketplace listing automatically, and the tool keeps running on new parts.
